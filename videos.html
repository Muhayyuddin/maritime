Project Videos

Demonstrations and documentation of our research progress

ROV Localization (Extended)

Extended testing of the ROV localization system under varying underwater conditions. This demonstration validates system robustness in different visibility levels, water currents, and operational depths for reliable autonomous navigation.

Line Tracking

Autonomous transect line following system for coral reef monitoring applications. The vision-based algorithm enables the ROV to follow predefined survey lines with high precision, ensuring consistent and repeatable monitoring data collection.

Line Tracking (Extended)

Extended validation of the transect line tracking system in various underwater conditions. This test demonstrates the algorithm's adaptability to different lighting conditions, water turbidity levels, and line configurations for robust coral reef surveying.

3D Mapping

Demonstration of underwater 3D environment reconstruction using advanced depth sensors and SLAM algorithms. This technology enables precise mapping of underwater structures, coral reefs, and seabed topography for comprehensive maritime monitoring.

Field Data Collection

Real-world field operations showcasing data acquisition from marine environments. This includes sensor deployment, environmental sampling, and collection of underwater imagery for training AI models and validating system performance.

ROV Localization

Demonstration of the Remotely Operated Vehicle positioning system in underwater environments. The localization algorithm combines acoustic positioning, visual odometry, and IMU data to achieve accurate real-time position tracking without GPS.

Underwater Maritime Monitoring

A joint initiative funded and spearheaded by Dubai Future Foundation, in collaboration with Khalifa University
