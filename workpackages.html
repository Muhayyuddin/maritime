Work Packages

Five comprehensive work packages covering all aspects of the project

WP1

Development of Multi-Modal Underwater ROV & USV

Design, development, and integration of robotic platforms, including mechanical and electrical systems, communication architecture, and sensor integration.

Key Activities:

  • Mechanical and electrical design of ROVs and USVs
  • Communication and control architecture development
  • Onboard computing system integration
  • Sensor integration and calibration
  • Waterproofing and environmental protection

Deliverables:

  • ROV prototype with integrated sensors
  • USV platform with communication systems
  • Technical specifications document
  • Integration test reports

Strategic Alignment

Supports the Dubai Robotics and Automation (DRA) Program and the UAE’s maritime innovation goals by building locally developed underwater and surface robotic platforms.

Dubai Future Labs · DFF

Leads the design and hardware prototyping of the project’s ROV and USV platforms, from mechanical and electrical systems to onboard integration, and prepares them for field deployment and marine trials in UAE waters.

Khalifa University

Assists platform development with parallel underwater vehicles, used to test control methods such as fault-tolerant thrust allocation.

Results to date
  • 4 robotic platforms: 1 surface, 3 underwater
  • Fault-tolerant thrust allocation · IEEE Access 2025
  • LLM-assisted multi-ROV inspection · Ocean Engineering 2025
WP2

System Navigation for USV & ROV

Development of robust navigation capabilities including localization, guidance, and autonomous operation in challenging maritime environments.

Key Activities:

  • Localization and guidance algorithm development
  • GPS and inertial navigation system integration
  • Simulation-based testing of navigation algorithms
  • Field experiments for validation in real conditions
  • Obstacle avoidance and path planning

Deliverables:

  • Navigation software suite
  • Simulation environment and test cases
  • Field test validation reports
  • Performance benchmarking results

Strategic Alignment

Builds UAE capability in autonomous marine navigation, supporting the DRA Program and safe autonomous operations in the UAE’s ports and coastal waters.

Dubai Future Labs · DFF

Integrates the navigation software on the ROV and USV platforms and supports its testing at sea in UAE waters.

Khalifa University

Develops the navigation software system: localization, guidance, path-following control and collision avoidance for the USV and ROV.

Results to date
  • Adaptive MPC path following · IEEE JOE 2026
  • CC-CBF collision avoidance · under review
  • EchoFactor sonar state estimation · under review
  • LLM-guided mission planning · under review
WP3

System Perception

Advanced perception and understanding of underwater environments through image processing, object detection, and multi-sensor data fusion.

Key Activities:

  • Underwater image and video denoising and enhancement
  • Object detection and tracking of marine structures and assets
  • Multi-sensor data fusion for reliable scene understanding
  • AI-based pattern recognition and classification
  • Real-time processing optimization

Deliverables:

  • Computer vision algorithms and models
  • Multi-sensor fusion framework
  • Object detection and tracking system
  • Performance evaluation datasets

Strategic Alignment

Advances UAE capability in AI-driven marine monitoring, supporting the DRA Program and the UAE’s priorities for protecting coral reefs, seagrass and mangroves.

Dubai Future Labs · DFF

Integrates perception sensors on the platforms and supports data collection during field visits in UAE waters.

Khalifa University

Develops the perception system: underwater image enhancement, object detection and tracking, and automated coral bleaching assessment.

Results to date
  • 4 benchmark datasets: MVTD, MUOT, FishDet-M, Transect Line
  • 2 invention disclosures: neuromorphic vision, chart-free bleaching assessment
  • Coral-CRCA · Marine Pollution Bulletin 2026
  • Maritime tracking benchmark · Pattern Recognition 2026
WP4

Test and Validation

End-to-end validation of the complete system through comprehensive testing, simulation campaigns, and real-world field trials.

Key Activities:

  • Simulation campaigns under different operational scenarios
  • Controlled field trials in UAE waters
  • Performance benchmarking and system evaluation
  • System refinement and optimization
  • Integration testing and validation

Deliverables:

  • Comprehensive test plan and procedures
  • Simulation test results and analysis
  • Field trial reports and findings
  • System performance evaluation

Strategic Alignment

Validates the system against the operational needs targeted by the Dubai Robotics and Automation (DRA) Program and the UAE’s maritime innovation goals.

Dubai Future Labs · DFF

Leads field deployment and marine trials in UAE waters, from trial planning and platform readiness to at-sea operations, including trials off the Dubai shore.

Khalifa University

Validates the navigation and perception systems in field trials, including vision-based transect following for coral reef surveys.

Results to date
  • 7 field visits to 4 sites
  • US provisional patent: diver-replacement transect survey system
  • ROV coral reef monitoring · ACRA 2025
  • CLIP-driven transect following · ROBOVISION 2026
WP5

Dissemination & Technology Transfer

Sharing project outcomes and maximizing impact through publications, demonstrations, and technology transfer activities.

Key Activities:

  • Scientific and technical publication preparation
  • Demonstrations to stakeholders, industry, and policymakers
  • Patent applications for key technologies and methods
  • Technology transfer and commercialization support
  • Conference presentations and workshops

Deliverables:

  • Peer-reviewed scientific publications
  • Patent applications and IP documentation
  • Stakeholder demonstration materials
  • Technology transfer documentation

Strategic Alignment

Advances the Dubai Robotics and Automation (DRA) Program and Dubai’s vision for future mobility and autonomous systems.

Dubai Future Labs · DFF

DFF leads the scaling of project outcomes into Dubai’s maritime and port ecosystem. DFL researchers co-author the project’s publications and are co-inventors on its IP filings.

Khalifa University

Drives academic publishing, benchmark datasets and PhD training, and files the project’s IP through KU-IP.

Results to date
  • 27 publications, 11 published
  • 3 patents & IP
  • 3 PhD theses
  • 2nd Best Poster · MARIS-AI
